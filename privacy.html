ADPAI privacy

Updated 17 October 2026. ADPAI is a prototype for checking food-label text for selected allergens. Uncertain reads remain unverified. Results do not guarantee safety.

Photos and processing

The current website and Capacitor app process selected photos on your device with Tesseract.js. They do not upload photos to an ADPAI analysis server. Photos and results are held in memory for the current session. Leaving or reloading the page clears them. The app can use the camera or the system photo picker only when you choose these actions. It does not deliberately save camera photos to your photo library. The operating system or camera provider may retain temporary files under its own rules.

Local storage

Your selected allergen profile and language preference are saved in this browser or app's local storage. The website caches its application files so it can reopen offline. Tesseract may cache downloaded language models locally. Photos and analysis results are not put in the website's offline cache. Clear this site's browser data, or remove the native app, to remove its local data. Browser and app profiles are separate.

Network services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its OCR runtime and language models are downloaded from jsDelivr. These providers receive ordinary network request information such as your IP address. The native app bundles its OCR runtime and Korean, English and French models; additional language models may still be downloaded from jsDelivr. Neither model downloads nor hosting requests intentionally include your photos or allergen profile. No advertising or analytics SDK is included in the shipped app.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the ADPAI project issue tracker. It is public: do not include photos, medical details or other personal information in an issue.
