ADPAI privacy

Updated 29 October 2026. ADPAI is a prototype for checking food-package labels and printed restaurant-menu descriptions for selected ingredients, with a separate cosmetics and personal-care vocabulary preview. Cosmetic explanations do not assess clinical allergy risk. The cosmetic vocabulary is incomplete and awaits independent review. Menus may omit ingredients and cross-contact risks; confirm with restaurant staff. Uncertain reads remain unverified. Results do not guarantee safety.

Photos and processing

The website and iOS app use Tesseract.js on your device. The Android app uses bundled Google ML Kit Korean and Latin text recognizers when its native bridge is available; the browser engine remains available on other platforms. They do not upload photos to an ADPAI analysis server. Photos and results are held in memory for the current document session, including a page the browser restores from its back/forward cache. A full reload or destruction of that document discards them; the browser may restore a completed result when you return from a viewer or share sheet. The app can use the camera or the system photo picker only when you choose these actions. It does not deliberately save camera photos to your photo library. The operating system or camera provider may retain temporary files under its own rules.

On supported browsers, live camera frames are checked locally for capture guidance. A full-resolution still photo is used when available; unsupported browsers retain the system camera/photo picker. The camera is closed when you cancel, take a photo, leave the page or put it in the background. Live frames, quality checks and page-corner edits are not uploaded to ADPAI. Image-quality hints do not verify ingredient completeness or food safety.

Cosmetics preview

Cosmetic photos and pasted label text stay in session memory. This separate page uses the on-device Korean/English Tesseract reader on both web and native packages. Its language choice is saved under a separate local key; the food-allergy profile is not read or modified. No external translator, language-model API or image API receives the label. Ingredient-name matches do not predict contact allergy, food allergy, impurity content or personal suitability.

Local storage

Your selected allergen profile and language preference are saved in this browser or app's local storage. The website caches its application files so it can reopen offline. Tesseract may cache downloaded language models locally. Photos and analysis results are not put in the website's offline cache. Clear this site's browser data, or remove the native app, to remove its local data. Browser and app profiles are separate.

Network services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its OCR runtime and language models are downloaded from jsDelivr. These providers receive ordinary network request information such as your IP address. The native app bundles its OCR runtime and Korean, English and French models; additional language models may still be downloaded from jsDelivr. Neither model downloads nor hosting requests intentionally include your photos or allergen profile. ADPAI does not add advertising or behavioral analytics. The Android ML Kit SDK may collect operational and diagnostic information under Google’s ML Kit terms. Selected photos and your allergy profile are not intentionally included in ADPAI network requests.

개인정보 처리 요약

사진과 선택한 알레르기 성분은 기기에서 처리됩니다. ADPAI 분석 서버로 사진을 업로드하지 않습니다. 웹과 iOS 앱은 Tesseract.js를, Android 앱은 사용 가능한 경우 기기에 포함된 Google ML Kit 한국어·라틴 문자 인식기를 사용합니다. 선택한 성분과 언어는 기기 내 저장소에 저장됩니다. 사진과 분석 결과는 현재 세션의 메모리에 보관되며 웹 오프라인 캐시에 저장되지 않습니다. Google ML Kit SDK는 Google 약관에 따라 운영·진단 정보를 수집할 수 있습니다. 결과는 안전을 보장하지 않으며 원본 라벨 또는 식당 직원에게 확인해야 합니다.

지원되는 브라우저에서는 실시간 카메라 화면을 기기에서만 확인해 촬영을 안내합니다. 취소·촬영 완료·페이지 이탈·백그라운드 전환 시 카메라를 종료합니다. 실시간 화면, 사진 품질 검사, 페이지 모서리 편집을 ADPAI 서버로 보내지 않습니다. 미지원 브라우저에서는 시스템 카메라나 사진 선택기를 사용합니다. 품질 안내는 음식의 안전을 확인하지 않습니다.

Contact
